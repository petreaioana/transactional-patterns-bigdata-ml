## Publication-safe derived datasets and analytical results

A separate open-data licence has not been granted for the publication-safe derived datasets and derived analytical result tables.

Until written permission is obtained from the data owner and a separate data licence is formally selected, no licence is granted for the reuse, redistribution, modification, or commercial exploitation of these dataset and result files.

The files affected by this notice include:

- `data/public/items_public_safe.csv`
- `data/public/baskets_public_safe.csv`
- `data/public/product_dictionary_public.csv`
- analytical result tables under `results/public/`
- analytical result tables under `results/core_reproduction/`
- analytical result tables under `results/bdcc_robustness/`

The files are included for scientific transparency, manuscript review, and reproducibility assessment. Their presence in the repository must not be interpreted as a transfer of ownership or as an unrestricted grant of reuse rights.

## Resources excluded from public licensing

No licence is granted for raw FoxPro DBF databases, FoxPro index files, original transaction-level records, original POS product labels, private product-name mappings, customer or event descriptions, waiter/operator/table identifiers, internal organizational identifiers, private-review files, backups, or local operational exports.

## Data-owner authorization

Public dissemination and any broader reuse of the derived datasets remain subject to the rights and authorization of the data owner. After written authorization for an open-data licence is obtained, this notice may be replaced by a separate data licence selected by the authors and approved by the data owner.
